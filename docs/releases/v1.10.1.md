## Summary

Superkeet v1.10.1 makes Actions Mode act sooner and type reliably. The last
step of a command now runs right after the sentence ends instead of after the
whole command finishes, and typing after ⌘N waits for the new window. The
bundled speech engine is unchanged.

## What's Changed

### Actions run sooner

- An app you ask to open runs as soon as Superkeet clearly hears its name, in
  any part of the command. Before, only the first step got this.
- The last step of a command, such as a shortcut, a web search, or typing, runs
  once the sentence ends and the speech engine has heard at least 300 ms of
  silence. Before, it waited for the end of the whole command.
- A web page waits until its sentence ends, because a browser name may still
  follow ("open x.com in Safari" opens it once, in Safari).
- Typing that ran early is never typed twice or deleted. If the final words
  extend it, only the rest is typed. If they differ, nothing more is typed, and
  the Actions window says what was typed early.

### Typing after a new window

- When a command ran after you stopped speaking, "create a new document and
  make the title say hello" could press ⌘N and type before the new window
  existed, so the text was lost while the Actions window said it was typed.
  With Instant App Launch turned off, it happened in every test run. Superkeet now waits
  for the new window, up to 1 second, before it types.
- In an app that makes the new item inside its current window, such as Notes,
  that wait runs the full second when the step runs after you stop speaking.

## Speech engine

Bundles [parakeet-cli v0.1.12](https://github.com/lucataco/parakeet-cli/releases/tag/v0.1.12)
(daemon protocol 2), unchanged from v1.10.0.

## Validation

- A test rig plays synthesized speech into the app through a BlackHole
  loopback and reads what appears on screen. For a three-sentence TextEdit
  command, over 12 runs of each build:
  - "hello" was typed a median 757 ms after it was said, against 1891 ms in
    v1.10.0;
  - the new document appeared a median 936 ms after "document?", against
    1160 ms.
- With Instant App Launch off, "hello" reached the document in 4 of 4 runs,
  against 0 of 4 before this fix.
- Each change was verified by a separate reviewer who ran it against the
  previous build before it merged.

## Install / Upgrade

Download `Superkeet-1.10.1.zip` from this release and move Superkeet to
Applications. Release packaging signs and notarizes the app and includes a
SHA-256 checksum file.

Homebrew updates become available after the tap has been synchronized:

```bash
brew update
brew upgrade --cask lucataco/tap/superkeet
```
