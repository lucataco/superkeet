## Summary

Superkeet v1.11.2 stops recordings from making your other audio quieter when
you wear Bluetooth headphones such as AirPods, and keeps Superkeet off your
playback device while it records. The bundled speech engine is unchanged.

## What's Changed

### Bluetooth headphones keep their volume and quality

- When an app opens a Bluetooth headset's microphone, macOS switches the
  headset to its call mode, which plays music and video quieter and at lower
  quality. With the microphone set to System Default, Superkeet did this on
  every recording while AirPods were connected.
- With System Default, Superkeet now records from the Mac's built-in
  microphone whenever the default input is a Bluetooth device. To record from
  the headphones' microphone, pick them under Settings → Microphone.
- When you connect or disconnect headphones, Superkeet restarts its speech
  engine so the next recording uses the right microphone. If a recording is in
  progress, the restart waits for it to finish.

### Recording no longer touches your output device

- Superkeet used to capture through a combined device made of the microphone
  and your current output device, set up one second after launch, and it
  started the output device on every recording. It now opens the microphone
  alone.

## Speech engine

Bundles [parakeet-cli v0.1.13](https://github.com/lucataco/parakeet-cli/releases/tag/v0.1.13)
(daemon protocol 2), unchanged from v1.11.1.

## Validation

- Measured on a MacBook Pro with the app's own capture code: before this
  change a recording ran both the microphone and the output device; after it,
  only the microphone.
- The Bluetooth change was not tested with headphones connected. The rule that
  picks the microphone is covered by unit tests.
- The automated suite passes (1105 tests, 17 opt-in tests skipped).

## Install / Upgrade

Download `Superkeet-1.11.2.zip` from this release and move Superkeet to
Applications. Release packaging signs and notarizes the app and includes a
SHA-256 checksum file.

Homebrew updates become available after the tap has been synchronized:

```bash
brew update
brew upgrade --cask lucataco/tap/superkeet
```
