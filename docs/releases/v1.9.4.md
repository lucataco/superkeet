## Summary

Superkeet v1.9.4 makes the app easier to understand for everyday dictation.
Setup now reports what is really going on, status lines and errors use plain
words instead of internal terms, and Settings puts the microphone where people
look for it. The speech engine is unchanged from v1.9.3.

## What's Changed

### Setup

- The last step no longer says "Setup Still Needs Attention" while the speech
  model downloads normally. It says "Almost ready" and shows the progress.
- Missing Accessibility no longer reads "You're All Set!". Setup explains that
  shortcuts need Accessibility and how to record from the menu bar until then.
- Setup asks for microphone and Accessibility access only when you click their
  buttons, and no longer opens system prompts on its own.
- Welcome mentions the model download only when the model is missing. The last
  step points to the waveform icon in the menu bar.
- An unavailable Apple Intelligence no longer makes dictation setup look broken.

### Status and errors

- The menu says "Dictation stopped. Open Settings for details." only when
  dictation can't work. A partial take, a recording that hit the time limit, or
  a cancelled paste no longer leaves that message behind.
- Without Accessibility, the menu reads "Shortcuts are off. Turn on
  Accessibility."
- Most messages that said "speech engine", "daemon", "Parakeet", "socket", or
  "segments" now say what happened and what to do, for example "Dictation took
  more than 20 seconds to start." Most exit codes, paths, and segment counts moved
  to the app log. Some startup and model download messages still show a path,
  an exit code, or the engine's own output for troubleshooting.
- A partial take shows "Some speech missed" in the overlay and "Incomplete" in
  History. With Remove Filler Words on, a take that is only filler words, such
  as "Um, uh.", reads "No speech detected" and pastes nothing.

### Settings

- The microphone picker moved from Advanced to General, after Shortcuts.
- Diagnostics moved from General to a Troubleshooting section at the end of
  Advanced, with the same Refresh, Copy Diagnostics, Export Logs, and engine
  restart buttons.
- The sidebar shows the version under the app name, so it stays visible at the
  smallest window size. About shows the version once and wraps its text instead
  of cutting it off.

### Menu and History

- "Copy Original Transcript" is enabled only when cleanup actually changed the
  text.
- History rows drop the placeholder icon and show ages such as "2 months ago".
  When history is off, the window offers a Turn On History button.

## Speech engine

Bundles [parakeet-cli v0.1.11](https://github.com/lucataco/parakeet-cli/releases/tag/v0.1.11)
(daemon protocol 2), unchanged from v1.9.3.

## Install / Upgrade

Download `Superkeet-1.9.4.zip` from this release and move Superkeet to
Applications. Release packaging signs and notarizes the app and includes a
SHA-256 checksum file.

Homebrew updates become available after the tap has been synchronized:

```bash
brew update
brew upgrade --cask lucataco/tap/superkeet
```
